3 · IOD seesaw rig + ocean boxes

Balance rig with twin tanks + dial gauge. Tilt the DMI — west/east levels oppose, needle follows. The map's IOD layer shows the same two boxes in the real ocean.

Fig 2IOD SEESAW RIG · DMI = W − E
Hint: + = positive IOD (cold east) · heat waves ride pIOD flanks—

Truth: DMI = SSTA west minus east (Saji 1999). pIOD 1982/91/94/97/2006 · nIOD 1980/81/85/92. ~50% co-occur ENSO; SETIO feedback, Kelvin/Rossby waves. 569-589.pdf p569–577. Cold tongue off Sumatra/Java, warm west → floods E Africa, EQUINOO explains ISMR extremes.

4 · Heat / cold threshold dial

Dial indicator on a stand. Set temperature + departure — needle classifies per IMD Tables 2.1/2.2, lamp lights the Core Zone shown on the map layer.

Fig 3THRESHOLD DIAL INDICATOR
Hint: plains HW ≥40 °C + dep +4–5 °C · needs 2 stations × 2 days—

Truth: HW plains ≥40/coast 37/hills 30; SHW dep >6.4 °C or ≥45/47 °C. CW dep −4.5…−6.4 °C. Core HW Zone N/NW/central/east (+coastal AP); CCZ ≈ same +J&K minus coastal AP. Cold −0.36/dec, heat duration +1 d/dec. Cold_Heat_Waves.pdf p18–48.
