# Images in this folder

All three files here are **Bureau of Meteorology (Australia)** diagrams, not
original artwork.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `iod-positive.png` | Indian Ocean Dipole, positive phase |
| `iod-negative.png` | Indian Ocean Dipole, negative phase |
| `iod-neutral.png` | Indian Ocean Dipole, neutral phase |

Each image carries **"© Commonwealth of Australia 2013"** printed on itself.

## Rights

Bureau of Meteorology publishes under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
Australia** (`bom.gov.au/copyright`). That licence permits copying, distribution
and adaptation **provided the work is attributed**. There is no
NonCommercial or NoDerivatives restriction on these diagrams.

## Required attribution

If you publish any of these, the credit must be **visible on the page** and must
say all of:

```
Indian Ocean Dipole diagrams: Bureau of Meteorology, © Commonwealth of Australia 2013,
used under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Australia (CC BY 3.0 AU).
```

Naming the file or the folder is not enough, and a credit buried only in a
source list at the bottom of the page is a weak reading of "attribution" —
keep it next to the image.

## Current status

**These three files are not referenced anywhere in the site.** They were found by
the copyright audit (`COPYRIGHT-AUDIT.md`, finding F2) as orphans: they would
have been published, would have earned nothing, and would have carried a
third-party copyright line with no visible credit for it.

They have been left in place rather than deleted, because they are reusable and
properly licensed. Two ways to close the finding, both fine:

- **Use them** — put them in the IOD section with the credit above.
- **Exclude them** — add `photos/` to `.gitignore` so they stay local. Nothing on
  the site breaks.
